\section{Post-fix Experimental Status}
\label{sec:experiments}

The first implementation of this study produced several numerical tables before the activation
state machine and the measurement contract had been audited.  Those tables are deliberately not
included in this submission: they used the pre-fix state machine, mixed incompatible cost units,
or estimated saturated marginal gains with too few Monte-Carlo trials.  Keeping invalid tables in
the paper would make them easy to quote as results and would also obscure the corrected evidence.

The valid post-fix regime sweep is reported in Section~\ref{sec:regime}.  It uses paired
Monte-Carlo trials at $\mathrm{MC}=300$, reports the seed fraction $|S|/n$, and records the
per-candidate paired standard error.  It establishes the regime-level sign pattern and corrects
the earlier negative-share claim.

The sequential selection and sample-cost comparison is intentionally left as an open experiment.
Before it can support a sample-efficiency claim, it must use the frozen contract, charge state
acquisition, repeat candidate pools, include degree, $\delta_2$, Full-MC and random-pruning
controls, and report paired quality and oracle cost.  The corresponding artifact and protocol are
listed in the project research state; no pre-fix baseline number is used here.

This separation is important: the analytic coverage counterexample and the corrected regime
characterisation are current results, whereas a post-fix quality--cost result is not yet
established.
