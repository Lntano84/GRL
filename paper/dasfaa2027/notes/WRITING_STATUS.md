## Read this first

Two findings on 2026-09-18 invalidated most of the numerical record. They are not stylistic issues;
each one changed a conclusion.

**1. The state machine was wrong.** Until commit `0248612`, a positively activated node could never
turn negative, and a node with `δ = θ^τ = 1` was denied activation. The fix removes 60–63% of the
measured spread on Congress-Twitter (k=1: 299.35 → 111.44; k=5: 358.52 → 137.28; k=10: 365.95 →
143.43). Every spread, marginal gain, calibration and robustness number in the draft was produced
with the broken machine. The draft marks them `[WITHDRAWN]`; they are kept, not deleted.

**2. The draft cited rank correlations from an `MC = 12` run.** `notes/CLAIMS.md` C2 warned in
writing that those correlations came from `mc-runs = 12` and that only the sign pattern may be used;
`docs/GATE3A_REPORT.md` records the command and repeats the warning. The abstract, the introduction
and the regime section quoted the magnitudes anyway, against the draft's own constraint that a rank
correlation in this regime requires `MC ≥ 300`.

A re-measurement at `MC = 300` under the fixed model (`scripts/audit/rederive_signflip_high_mc.py`,
output `docs/results/signflip_fixedmodel_mc300.json`) gives, for Congress-Twitter at
`|S|/n = 20%`:

| quantity | draft (MC=12, pre-fix) | re-measured (MC=300, fixed) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| negative-marginal share | 44.0% | **4.0%** (0.0% beyond noise) |
| `ρ_degree` | −0.112 | **+0.039** |
| `ρ_δ₂` | +0.034 | **+0.030** |

The headline phenomenon — a large share of candidates with negative marginal, and `δ₂` separating
from degree — is not visible in that cell at adequate power.

## What the paper can defend today

Only the analytic material survives the two findings:

1. **The objective has no non-negative seed-independent coverage representation.** Exact one-hop
   arithmetic: `F_D(∅) = 0.0000`, `F_D({a}) = 0.4800`, `F_D({a,b}) = 0.3200` under the model's
   simplex law, versus `0.0000 / 0.4000 / 0.8000` under uniform-threshold LT. Closed form
   `2δ(1−δ)` validated against 2-D numerical integration (worst difference `4.5e-4`).
2. **The threshold law must be named**, because the sign of the effect depends on it.
3. **`λ` is a difference of two submodular functions**, so even a valid upper bound would not
   license a sampling guarantee.
4. **A measurement-protocol constraint**: no rank correlation in the negative-marginal regime below
   `MC ≥ 300`.

That is a short paper. It is also the only part that is currently true.

* That density causes the sign flip.
* That degree is universally harmful (NetHEPT is a counterexample).
* That the source model's Theorem 6 is false.
* That the method beats OPIM-C, or any claim that RR machinery is unavailable for the *upper bound*.
* Absolute spread comparisons between the overexposure model and independent cascade: the two are
  not on a common scale (the overexposure model's percolation scale is far above IC's on the same
  graph). Any statement of this kind must be re-measured; the earlier comparison is withdrawn.
