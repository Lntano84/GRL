# GRID08 changes

- PRE01, before simulation: selected unavailable names from entire archive split. Retained design_v0_UNRUN.json and switched to the original extracted monthly validation list; no outcome-based selection.
- PRE02, before simulation: terminal penalty set to 1000 to discourage early termination as a way to avoid operational cost. Design revision retained.
- INPUT01, before training: MLP also receives padded graph endpoints, giving both encoders access to the same legal connectivity information. Parameter counts are reported separately.
- LOG01: three first runner attempts stopped after one physical step each on NumPy float32 JSON serialization, before any PPO update. Entire v0_LOGGING_FAILED directory, original source and logs retained. Fixed serialization and reran unchanged learning definitions under v0. Failed attempts are excluded from performance comparisons and included in total execution accounting.

Original author assets remain read-only. Later model versions must use separate directories and retain v0 checkpoints.

- MODEL02 (V2): V0 finished four validation weeks and reproduced ALWAYS_RESTORE exactly; V1 GNN developed a predominantly no-restoration policy. Preserve both. V2 loads each V1 checkpoint, adds a training-only bias shift toward median restoration probability 0.95, retains its critic and state-dependent logit differences, resets Adam, halves the learning rate, and performs four additional passes over the same training weeks. GNN and MLP receive the same schedule. This is development policy initialization/continued training, not evidence of novelty and not an independent confirmation. Prior-shift, critic invariance, source hashes and inactive masks passed before fits. The cumulative development budget is extended before execution to 220,000 physical steps (planned worst case 202,343), with original per-process wall and output-byte caps retained. Final test is not used for tuning or scoring.

- MASK01 (V1 engineering): first V1 GNN attempt stopped at its first 256-step update before optimizer execution, because Torch 2.8 cannot construct this Boolean tensor directly from NumPy 2.4 Boolean scalars. Entire run and original runner/freeze are preserved in v1_MASK_FAILED. Convert each condition to native bool and add a native NumPy-feature mask precheck. No learning definition, reward, candidate or split changed. Include 256 failed-attempt physical steps in total consumption.

- MODEL01 (V1): training-only V0 diagnostics found about 7% of rollout steps offered a residual decision and near-constant deterministic actor output. Created separate pilot_v1.py and networks_v1.py: offered-only policy loss/normalization, independent common critic, proposal-invariant critic bootstrap, separately clipped gradients. Eight targeted neural checks passed before training. V0 learning definitions and all V0 artifacts are unchanged; V1 has its own design and code hashes. Reward, candidate screens, legal inputs and week selection remain unchanged. The cumulative physical-step budget for the two-version development pilot is explicitly extended to 150,000 before V1 runs; the planned full-horizon maximum is about 121,400 including V0 evaluation. Actual consumption must be reconciled at delivery. No final-test outcomes are used.

- DIAG01: replayed one V1 training failure without modifying any live model. The first 131 physical actions/observations matched their saved hashes. Public restoration→no-op forecast fails at step two while hold→no-op survives those two steps; separately marked privileged actual branches agree. This is a local counterexample, not a full-week safety or learning claim. First `from_vect` API attempt failed before physical stepping and is retained.
- GUARD01: separately qualified a common two-step public-forecast veto on two training weeks and two fixed rules. It did not change V2 or open evaluation/final-test data. Complete saved-vector audit passed. Both training weeks produced no veto; October survived and January failed at the same step with both rules. Therefore the local counterexample does not establish a general safety gain.
- RESUME01: three Python processes disappeared without a failure marker; exact external cause is unknown. Preserved the incomplete episode folders and original update logs. Separate resume runners restore the latest whole-episode model, Adam state and Torch RNG, retaining the frozen schedule; they do not resume midway through the electrical episode. 3,438 physical steps are logged in discarded partial prefixes, with a conservative upper bound of 3,441. These costs count in addition to successful completion. Per-invocation caps remain enforced but are not described as one uninterrupted wall-time cap. Original runners and design hashes remain unchanged.
